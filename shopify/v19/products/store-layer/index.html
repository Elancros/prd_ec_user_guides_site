    Send products to Shopify¶

    Odoo products go to Shopify in two steps:

    1. Add to Store Layer: prepares the products for a store and checks them. Nothing is sent yet.
    2. Export to Store: creates them on Shopify.

    Later changes in Odoo are sent with Update on Store.

    1. Add to Store Layer¶

    1. In Sales › Products › Products (or Inventory), select the products.
    2. Click Actions ⚙ › Add to Store Layer.
    3. Choose the Stores. The Status column shows, for each product and store, whether it can be added and why not.
    4. Optional: tick Also Add Blocked Products to add products that cannot be exported yet, to fix them later.
    5. Click Confirm.

    The products are added with all their variants and images, and get the store's Default Sales Channels.

    Ready or Blocked¶

    Each layer product is Ready (can be exported) or Blocked. The Layer Messages say why:

    Reason Fix
    Variants have no SKU / barcode (the code used by Match Product By) Give them one in Odoo.
    Two variants have the same identifier Give each variant its own SKU or barcode.
    An identifier is already used by another product of this store Correct the duplicate.
    A variant is already linked to another product of this store Unlink it from the other product.
    The product belongs to another company, or has no active variant Not possible: the product is not added.

    After fixing, select the products and click Actions ⚙ › Re-check.

    Why a check before sending

    Shopify finds a variant by its SKU or barcode, and so does every later import and order. A product sent without codes, or with a code another product already uses, would be matched to the wrong product later. The check catches it before anything reaches your store.

    Attributes that create no variants

    Attributes set to never create variants in Odoo are not sent to Shopify: a warning lists them.

    2. Export to Store¶

    1. Open Shopify › Products › Product Templates and select the Ready products.
    2. Click Actions ⚙ › Export to Store and confirm.
    • The products are created on Shopify with their variants and images.
    • If Shopify already has a product with the same SKU or barcode, that product is linked instead of created twice.
    • For products already on Shopify, only their new variants are added.

    Safe to repeat

    Each product created on Shopify is followed as a store request: if Odoo gets no answer, the next run asks Shopify before sending it again, so a product is never created twice.

    3. Update on Store¶

    When a value ticked Sync on Update (Export) in Field Mappings changes in Odoo (e.g. the name or the description), the layer product gets Needs Update.

    Way How
    By hand Select the products in Product Templates, Actions ⚙ › Update on Store. Sends the title, description, prices, variants and images, and the other values ticked Sync on Update (Export).
    Scheduler Add Update Products on Store in Schedulers and switch it on (at most once an hour). Each run sends only the products marked Needs Update.

    Other store layer actions¶

    All in Product Templates › Actions ⚙; each opens a window that shows what will happen to each product first.

    Action What it does
    Refresh from Odoo Copies the title, description and category from the Odoo products again and adds their new variants and images. Nothing is sent: use Update on Store afterwards.
    Re-check Checks blocked products again after you fixed them.
    Set to Mapping Only The products stay linked (orders still find them) but are no longer exported, updated or imported.
    Enable Sync Mapping Only products are exported and updated again, with all their variants.
    Set Stock Settings Track Inventory and the Stock Export Rule of the variants: Actual Qty, Fixed Quantity Cap or Percentage of Available Stock, with its Rule Value.

    Archiving a product in Odoo

    Archiving an Odoo product that is linked to a store does not change it on Shopify: it stays on sale there. Odoo warns you; to stop selling it, change it on Shopify.

    The layer product¶

    Open a product in Product Templates to see and change its store values:

    Part What it holds
    Store Title The product name on Shopify. It can differ from the Odoo name.
    Odoo Product Template The Odoo product this store product is linked to.
    External Product ID, External Reference / Handle Shopify's ID and the product's address on the store. The arrow next to the ID opens the product in Shopify admin.
    Mode Synced (exported, updated and imported) or Mapping Only (only linked, so orders find it).
    Sync Status, Exported to Store, Last Sync Date Where the product stands with Shopify, e.g. Synced, or waiting to be sent after a change in Odoo.
    Published on Store Active (on) or draft (off) on Shopify.
    Variant Bridges tab Each variant with its Shopify ID, SKU and barcode, Track Inventory and its Stock Export Rule.
    Images, Store Categories, Sales Channels tabs The images sent or received, the store categories, and the channels the product is published on.
    Store Description, Short Description tabs The texts shown on Shopify, if they differ from Odoo's.

    The Variants button at the top opens the store variants of the product.
