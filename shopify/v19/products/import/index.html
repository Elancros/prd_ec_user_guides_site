    Import products¶

    Three operations bring Shopify products into Odoo, and two ways keep them up to date afterwards.

    Operation Use it for
    Import Products All products created or changed in a period: the first import, or catching up.
    Import Specific Product One product, now.
    Map Products from File Linking Odoo products to Shopify products when their codes do not match.

    Import Products¶

    1. Open the store (or its card on the Dashboard) and click Perform Operation.
    2. Choose Import Products. The blue box explains what will happen.
    3. Choose Date Based On, From Date and To Date: Updated Date takes the products created or changed in the period, Created Date only those created in it.
    4. Click Execute.

    What Odoo does with each Shopify product:

    Case Result
    New product, with a status ticked in Product Statuses to Import Each variant looks for its Odoo product by SKU or barcode (Match Product By).
    Variant found in Odoo It is linked. With Auto Update Product on, the values ticked Sync on Update (Import) in Field Mappings are updated.
    Variant not found With Auto Import Product on, the product is created. Off: it is reported in the sync job and skipped.
    Option (e.g. Size: XL) unknown in Odoo Created with Auto Create Attributes; otherwise the product waits for its Attribute Mapping.
    Product already in the store layer Its changes are applied, a new status too, whatever the statuses ticked.
    Product waiting to be sent to Shopify (Needs Update) Not overwritten: your Odoo changes win.

    What happens next

    The work runs in the background: follow it in Shopify › Activity › Sync Jobs. The products appear under Shopify › Products › Product Templates; their images, if switched on, a few minutes later.

    First import

    Import a short period first (e.g. the last week) and check the products, variants, attributes and categories created. Then import everything with a From Date before your store opened.

    Read the result¶

    Open the sync job of the import (Shopify › Activity › Sync Jobs): one line per product, with its state.

    Line state Meaning What to do
    Completed The product is linked, created or updated. Nothing.
    Skipped Nothing to do, e.g. no Odoo product found and Auto Import Product off. The message says why. Fix the code in Odoo or map the product, then import it again.
    Failed An error, e.g. an attribute to map. Retried in the background first. Read the message, fix the cause, then Retry Failed Lines on the job.

    Large imports¶

    When an import finds more products than Large Import From (Product Settings, default 1,000), Shopify prepares the list in the background and Odoo imports it in batches. Follow it in Bulk Imports.

    Import Specific Product¶

    Brings one Shopify product into Odoo at once, with the same rules as Import Products. Useful to test the settings on one product, or to bring a product a customer just ordered.

    1. In Shopify admin, open the product: its ID is the number at the end of the address (…/products/8012345678901).
    2. In Odoo, Perform Operation › Import Specific Product, paste the ID in Specific External ID and click Execute.

    If the product was deleted on Shopify, it is removed from the store layer; the Odoo product stays.

    Map Products from File¶

    Links Odoo products to Shopify products with a CSV or Excel file, when SKUs or barcodes do not match between Odoo and Shopify, or to link many products at once. Administrators only.

    1. Perform Operation › Map Products from File.
    2. Click Download Template, fill one row per variant and save the file.
    3. Upload it in File.
    4. Sync These Products:
      • Off (most common): the products are only linked (Mapping Only), so orders find them. They are never exported or updated.
      • On: they are added for export and update, with all their variants and the full checks.
    5. Click Execute. The result of every row is shown in the sync job.

    When to use it

    Your Odoo products use internal codes (CH-0042), your Shopify store uses supplier SKUs (ERG-CHAIR-BLK). Rather than changing either, list both in the file: Odoo links them, and orders of ERG-CHAIR-BLK use CH-0042.

    Keep products up to date automatically¶

    Way How
    Webhooks Switch on Product Created, Product Updated and Product Deleted and click Register Webhooks: Shopify tells Odoo about each change at once. See Webhooks.
    Scheduler Add Import Products in Schedulers and switch it on: each run reads what changed since the last one. See Schedulers.

    What happens next

    Send Odoo products to Shopify, or send your Odoo changes back: Send products to Shopify.
